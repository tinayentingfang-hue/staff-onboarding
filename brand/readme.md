# ISO — Skin Cancer & Laser Clinic

Brand design system for ISO Clinic (isoclinic.com.au), a doctor-led skin cancer, laser and cosmetic clinic in Upper Mount Gravatt, Brisbane. Founded by Dr Jack Fu and Dr Tina Fang. Tone: calm, precise, reassuring, clinical yet warm.

## Logo
Circular topographic/wood-grain ring around the ISO serif wordmark. Five lockups in `assets/logo/` plus a generated green one:
- `logo-black-on-white.png` — black mark on white
- `logo-white-on-green.png` — white on Green #999A88 (primary, matches website)
- `logo-white-on-pink.png` — white on Pink #F4A39E
- `logo-white-on-sand.png` — white on Sand #EBE7D8
- `logo-white-on-mist.png` — white on Mist #D1D9DF
- `logo-white-on-black.png` — white on Black #000000
Never recolor or redraw the mark; give it generous clear space (≥ the height of the "ISO" wordmark on all sides).

## Color
- Black `#000000` / Ink `#141414` (text)
- White `#FFFFFF`, Paper `#FAF9F5`
- Green `#999A88` (primary brand accent — the website's sage/army green; Green Deep `#6B6C5C` for text/links on light)
- Sage `#98A9A7` (cool grey-green accent/surface)
- Pink `#F4A39E` (secondary accent; Pink Deep `#C96F6A` for text/links on light)
- Sand `#EBE7D8`, Mist `#D1D9DF` (calm surface tints)
Backgrounds are light (White/Paper/Sand/Mist) or full Black; green leads, pink supports. Use ONLY Green #999A88 — no other greens.

## Type
- Display: Cormorant Garamond 500–600 (echoes the serif wordmark)
- Body/UI: Karla 400–600; UI labels often uppercase, letterspaced 0.08–0.12em

## Tokens
All in `styles.css` as `--iso-*` custom properties.
