## 2. Busy Hours

**What's urgent?**
- Anyone not safe, patient not happy, bleeding non-stop, pending script, pain, urgent result.

**When it's not urgent:** email Dr Tina Fang at tina.yenting.fang@gmail.com with subject "ISO Questions."

**During busy days**
1. Clean up rooms between patients.
2. Undress the patient for a full skin check in Room 1 — offer a blanket/drape for female patients only. Dr Jack positions patients face down; Dr Tina positions patients face up.
3. Help doctors clean up and get the next patient ready.

## 3. Cryotherapy Equipment Handling (Liquid Nitrogen)

- **Filling up:** wear gloves and eye protection.
- **Emptying out:** pour any leftover liquid back into the tank — be careful of the fragile stick.
- Use 1–2 scoops.

## 4. ISO Cleaning Protocols — Everyday Cleaning Tasks

Allocate at least 30 minutes each day for these essential duties.

**General**
- Wipe reception desk and workstations (baby wipes).
- Clean phones, earphones (if used), the Tyro machine + cord, keyboard, and mouse (alcohol wipes).
- Vacuum the floor thoroughly and empty the vacuum dustbin.
- Empty the rubbish bin and replace the liner.

**Waiting Room**
- Wipe the waiting room table and water station (baby wipes).
- Ensure paper cups are stocked and not overfilled.

**Consultation Rooms (Room 1 & 2)**
- Empty rubbish bins and replace liners.
- Check stock of gloves, tissues, and face wipes in treatment rooms.
- Wipe any visible blood stains.

Each day also has a deep-cleaning task assigned according to the day of the week, included in the daily task list.
