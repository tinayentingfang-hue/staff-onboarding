## 1. Main Types of Histology

- **Solar Keratosis:** a rough, scaly patch on sun-exposed skin caused by long-term UV damage — considered a precancerous lesion.
- **IEC / SCC:** Intraepidermal Carcinoma is an early, superficial form of Squamous Cell Carcinoma confined to the top layer of skin, whereas SCC can invade deeper tissue and potentially spread if not treated promptly.
- **BCC (Basal Cell Carcinoma):** the most common type of skin cancer — typically slow-growing and locally invasive, arising from the basal cells of the epidermis, and rarely spreads.
- **Melanoma:** a serious and potentially life-threatening form of skin cancer.

## 2. Handling Patient Calls About Results

When a patient calls to ask about histology results, it is generally best practice to **check with the doctors first before disclosing the result.**

However, if the patient appears anxious, and you have the report in front of you and are confident it clearly shows a benign result, you may gently reassure them: *"I have the report in front of me and I can see that it shows a benign result. I will still double-check this with the doctor for confirmation. Would you like a follow-up phone call once the doctor has reviewed it?"*

## 3. Benign Result Communication

If a patient calls with questions after receiving a benign-result message — especially if the histology terms are hard to pronounce — offer to email the result for their reference. This helps ensure clarity and reassurance.

## 8. Post-Procedure Protocols

**4 steps after a Full Skin Exam (FSE)**
1. Check if the patient is happy: "How did it go?"
2. Book in for the next FSE: "Let me put you on our 12-monthly/6-monthly list."
3. Payment: "Today is $150, how would you like to pay?"
4. Rebate: tick "Send Online" if paying by phone/Visa; tick "Instant Rebate" if a debit card is available.

**4 steps after a Spot Check**
1. Check if the patient is happy: "How did it go?"
2. Offer follow-ups: "Do you need to come back for a review?"
3. Payment: "Today is $100, how would you like to pay?"
4. Rebate: tick "Send Online" if paying by phone/Visa; tick "Instant Rebate" if a debit card is available.

**4 steps after a Biopsy**
1. Check if the patient is happy: "How did it go?"
2. Advise 7 days for results — we will message or call when results are available.
3. Ensure follow-up: we don't do "no news is good news" — ask the patient to call back next week if they've heard nothing.
4. Payment: $50 out of pocket for 1 biopsy, +$25 for each additional.

**4 steps after an Excision**
1. Give the patient the aftercare instruction sheet.
2. Book a wound review/ROS (Removal of Stitches) — ask the doctor when to book it.
3. Payment: excision fees are private fees, no GST, with a Medicare rebate.
4. Rebate: 7 days — print a receipt for the patient to chase with Medicare.

**4 steps after PDT**
1. Give aftercare instructions with the consent form.
2. Book a 1-month follow-up — offer any other day of the week.
3. Payment: $700, no GST, per area.
4. Private health rebate: usually no — only print an invoice if the patient asks about a private health rebate.

**4 steps after Cosmetic Mole Removal (CMR)**
1. Give the aftercare instructions sheet.
2. Book a 1-month follow-up.
3. Payment: $350 + $100 each additional; no extra fee unless not flat (pigmentation costs extra); billed to the GST doctor account.
4. Private health rebate: no — it is cosmetic.
